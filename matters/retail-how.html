How to bring online shoppers in: show what is in stock nearby

By ContentMatter · 3 min read · Published November 4, 2026

Shoppers search online before they walk in. If your listing shows what you have in stock and when they can pick it up, the search can end at your door.

Most sales still happen outside e-commerce

The U.S. Census Bureau reported that e-commerce made up 17.1% of total retail sales in the second quarter of 2026, seasonally adjusted. That leaves about 83 cents of every retail dollar outside e-commerce, which is why the store still matters. The Bureau has said revised estimates will come with its third quarter release on November 19, 2026, so check for the latest figure.

Show what is on your shelves

Google's free local listings let the in store products you sell appear across Google at no cost. Local inventory ads are the paid version, run through Google Ads. Both need a Merchant Center account and information about what each store has. Eligible retailers in the U.S., Canada, the U.K., and Ireland can use Google's local inventory app to add in store products automatically.

What Google says it delivers

Google reports that retailers who used local inventory ads alongside Shopping ads saw a 21% increase in store visits and a 9% increase in online conversions for products available in store, based on Google data from July 2023 to July 2024. That is Google's own figure, not an independent study, and results vary by store.

Offer pickup

Google lets retailers highlight products that can be reserved or bought online and picked up in store the same day or the next day. Pickup gives a shopper a reason to choose your store over a delivery wait, and it brings them through the door.

Keep the basics right

A listing that shows stock is only useful if your hours, phone number, address, and holiday hours are also right. Check them each quarter, and update them before every holiday.

Measure visits, calls, and pickups

Count calls, direction requests, and pickup orders each week. Where it is available to you, use store visit conversions in Google Ads. Compare weeks with and without the listings, and change one thing at a time.

An illustrative example

A neighborhood gift shop lists its best selling candles and cards with local availability. A shopper searches for a gift nearby, sees a candle in stock three blocks away with pickup today, and walks in. This is a made up scenario. The shop's own counts of pickups and calls show whether it happens often enough to keep investing.

Common mistakes to avoid

Listing products that are sold out, letting prices differ between the shelf and the listing, skipping hours updates, and paying for ads before the free listing is working.

A simple plan for the next 30 days

Week one, set up Merchant Center and check that your store is eligible. Week two, add your top twenty products with accurate stock and prices. Week three, turn on same day pickup if your team can handle it. Week four, count visits, calls, and pickups, then decide whether paid local inventory ads are worth testing.

Try this first

Search for your three best selling products plus your town on a phone. See whether your store appears and whether the listing shows stock.
