Questions

Before you book.

What is ContentMatter?

ContentMatter is a digital marketing agency for local businesses. We run Google Ads, Local SEO, and Google Business Profile work, set up call and form tracking, and document every campaign in a written Campaign Record, for food and beverage, retail, beauty, health and wellness, and home and commercial service businesses.

Which businesses do you work with?

Five groups: food and beverage, retail, beauty (med spas, salons, studios), health and wellness (dental, optical, chiropractic, wellness), and home and commercial services (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, cleaning, roofing).

Who owns the ad accounts and data?

You do. Ad accounts and analytics stay in your name, and you can leave with your data and your Campaign Record at any time.

Is there a long contract?

No lock in beyond the initial 90 day sprint.

What do I get in the free audit?

We review your ads, tracking, and landing pages and send a written summary in two business days. A short call walks through it, with no obligation.

How much does it cost?

Every client gets a custom quote after discovery. Campaign Sprints start from $1,500.

How will I see results?

A monthly report goes to your portal by the 7th, with a quarterly strategy review and a Campaign Record of every change and why it was made.
