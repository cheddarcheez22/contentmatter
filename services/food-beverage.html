The problem we solve

Visibility and repeat guests,
without clear numbers.

The National Restaurant Association reports that 42% of operators were not profitable last year and 60% saw softer customer traffic, so spend has to count. Yet most restaurant marketing runs on generic promotions, not on your slow shifts, your regulars, or the way guests actually find you. BentoBox found that 61% of diners who discover restaurants online start with a search engine, so your listing and the actions people take from it matter most.Sources: National Restaurant Association, 2026 State of the Restaurant Industry; BentoBox Diner Discovery Report via Restaurant Dive, 2023.

01

Calls

Phone orders and reservations by source. We count calls from your ads and listing, log which ones turn into orders or tables, and show cost per call by campaign.

02

Directions

Directions requests from search and maps. We track directions as a local action in Google Ads and your Business Profile, so you see which campaigns send people to your door.

03

Reservations

Reservation clicks tracked to the ad. We make each reservation link its own conversion and tag the source, including third party booking links that usually go untracked.

04

Online orders

Order clicks tracked by source. We separate online order clicks from calls and reservations and review them weekly, so menu and timing changes can be tied to results.

Questions from restaurants

Restaurant marketing, answered.

What does ContentMatter do for restaurants?

ContentMatter is a local marketing agency that runs Google Ads, Local SEO, Google Business Profile and Yelp listings, email and SMS, and guest retention programs for local restaurants, cafes, and food brands. We track calls, directions, reservation clicks, and online orders so you can see which marketing fills tables.

How do you know if marketing brings guests in?

We count the actions guests take before a visit: calls, direction requests, reservation clicks, and online orders, each tied to the ad, listing, or email that drove it. Your monthly report arrives by the 7th and shows which channels filled tables and what changed.

Can you help with delivery apps and my POS?

Yes. Delivery app launch and management covers listing and menu optimization, promotions, and reviews. POS Marketing Setup is a one to two week project that connects your POS to email, SMS, loyalty, ordering, and tracking.

How do I get first time guests to come back?

The Guest Retention Program uses email and SMS flows built on your own guest list to bring first time guests back for a second visit and keep regulars returning, with every return visit tracked by source.

How much does restaurant marketing cost?

Every client gets a custom quote after discovery, based on your goals and your monthly ad budget. Ad spend is paid directly to the ad platform. Campaign Sprints start from $1,500, and most restaurants begin with a free audit or a two to three week Paid Ads Launch. A Campaign Sprint fits a grand opening or a new menu.
