Questions from beauty businesses

Med spa and salon marketing, answered.

What does ContentMatter do for med spas and salons?

ContentMatter is a local marketing agency that runs Google Ads, Local SEO, Google Business Profile, review generation, and email and SMS for med spas, salons, nail studios, lash and brow bars, and skin clinics. We track every inquiry and booking back to its source.

How do you track consultations and bookings from ads?

We track each stage separately: inquiry, booked consultation, attended consultation, and paid treatment, each labeled by source. That shows your cost per new client by channel and where inquiries drop before a booking.

Do you send client health information to ad platforms?

No. We set up tracking so client health details stay out of ad and analytics tools, and we recommend confirming the approach with your compliance advisor.

How do you get more reviews for a med spa or salon?

We send a review request by email or text within a few days of the visit, ask every client, and never pay for or filter reviews. We also draft replies to Google and Yelp reviews for your approval, without sharing any client details.

How much does med spa marketing cost?

Every client gets a custom quote after discovery, based on your goals and your monthly ad budget. Ad spend is paid directly to the ad platform. Campaign Sprints start from $1,500, and most businesses begin with a free audit or a two to three week Paid Ads Launch.
