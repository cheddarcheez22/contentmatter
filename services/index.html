About our services.

What services does ContentMatter offer?

ContentMatter is a digital marketing agency for local businesses. Services include Local SEO and AI search visibility, Google Business Profile and review management, Google, Meta, and Yelp ads, websites and landing pages, GA4 and conversion tracking, email and SMS automation, Campaign Sprints, and a written Campaign Record for every campaign.

Which businesses does ContentMatter work with?

Five groups: food and beverage (restaurants, cafes, food brands), retail (local shops and brands), beauty (med spas, salons, studios), health and wellness (dental, optical, chiropractic, wellness clinics), and home and commercial services (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, cleaning, roofing).
