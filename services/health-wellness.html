Questions from health and wellness practices

Clinic and practice marketing, answered.

What does ContentMatter do for dental, optical, and wellness practices?

ContentMatter is a local marketing agency that runs Google Ads, Local SEO, Google Business Profile, review generation, and email and SMS for dental and orthodontic practices, optical shops, chiropractors, physical therapy, and wellness clinics. We track every call, form, and booking back to its source.

How do you track new patients from ads?

We track each stage separately: call or booking request, booked appointment, and attended first visit, each labeled by source. That shows your cost per new patient by channel and where requests drop before an appointment.

Do you send patient health information to ad platforms?

No. We set up call, form, and booking tracking so patient health details stay out of ad and analytics tools, and we recommend confirming the approach with your compliance advisor.

Can a dental or medical practice ask patients for reviews?

Medical and dental practices have extra review and advertising rules, so we handle review requests case by case. We never pay for or filter reviews, and replies never include patient details.

How much does dental or clinic marketing cost?

Every client gets a custom quote after discovery, based on your goals and your monthly ad budget. Ad spend is paid directly to the ad platform. Campaign Sprints start from $1,500, and most practices begin with a free audit or a two to three week Paid Ads Launch.
